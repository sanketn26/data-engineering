What to observe

  • Hot key ratio = 0%: Even distribution. Each consumer handles similar load. Lag stays low.
  • Hot key ratio > 50%: One partition receives most messages. One consumer falls behind. Others are idle. This is a hot partition.
  • Consumers > Partitions: Extra consumers are idle. Kafka assigns at most one consumer per partition per group.
  • Consumer speed > Producer rate: Lag never accumulates. Consumers keep up.
  • Consumer speed much lower than Producer rate: Lag grows. Scale consumers or reduce producer rate.
